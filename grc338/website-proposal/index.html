Introduction

Dough-Licious Donuts

Dough-Licious Donuts is a small business donut and coffee shop located in Claremont, California. Our donuts are made fresh everyday from scratch. They are made and sold in small batches to guarantee fresh donuts all day long. We serve all of the classic donuts along with fun new flavors that change every week! Grab a coffee to go with your donut. We have a variety of syrups including lavender, caramel, vanilla, and even cookie butter!

Target audience

Located just five minutes away from the Claremont Colleges, Dough-Licious Donuts is a great spot for students to grab a quick breakfast or refuel with coffee in the afternoon for a study session or class.

Whether you are headed out for a day of school or work, stop by and grab a coffee and satisfy your sweet treat craving. With our range of donuts, there is something for everyone to enjoy!

About

Dough-Licious Donuts opened in Claremont, California in 2023. After the Covid-19 pandemic a lot of people were feeling disconnected from one another. It became our mission to open up an inviting donut shop with lots of seating and colorful decorations to encourage customers to stop in and stay a while. We hope you stop by our shop and bring your friends and family. With our wide menu selection there's something for everyone to enjoy!
