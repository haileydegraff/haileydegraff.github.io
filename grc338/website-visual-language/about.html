Welcome to Dough-Licious Donuts!

Dough-Licious Donuts opened in Claremont, California in 2023. After the Covid-19 pandemic a lot of people were feeling disconnected from one another. It became our mission to open up an inviting donut shop with lots of seating and colorful decorations to encourage customers to stop in and stay a while.

We have a wide variety of donuts that are freshly made everyday. We offer all the classic donuts you love alongside brand new flavors each week!

We hope you stop by our shop and bring your friends and family. With our wide menu selection there's something for everyone to enjoy! Come visit our store on 265 Licoln Drive in Claremont or place an online order for delivery.
